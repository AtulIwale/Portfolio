# Synthetic dataset card

## Provenance and intended use

Every row is generated by `aec/data.py` with a seeded NumPy generator. No client data,
anonymised client rows, actual supplier identities or manual text was used. Fictional names
are deliberately obvious. This is not a faithful Xpedeon database/export schema or endorsed product.
The user's register-only constraint informs the design, not a claim of verified export availability.

## Register-inspired versus assumed

| Table | Grain and relationship | Basis / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| projects | One procurement project; `project_id` | Simplified project master; 30 fictional projects |
| suppliers | One supplier; `supplier_id` | Simplified external-entity master; 60 fictional suppliers |
| requisitions | One requisition line | Requisition-register concept; one line per request in this version |
| orders | One order line; FK requisition/project/supplier | Order-register concept; one line per order; no revision history |
| grns | One receipt against an order | GRN/rejection concepts; one or two receipts; rejected quantities retained separately |
| invoices | One invoice for one receipt | AP invoice-register concept; excludes tax, retention, credit notes and exchange differences |
| payments | One payment instalment | Payments-register concept; one or two instalments per invoice |
| packages | One standalone commercial project/package | Work-package concept; unique CP project IDs, separate from procurement P IDs |
| snapshots | Three staged observations per package | **Fictional extension**: physical progress, costs and remaining commitments are scenario inputs |
| variations | One variation with current synthetic status | Contract-variation concept; status history is not modelled |
| outcomes | One completed package | **Fictional ground truth**: final cost and completion date generated for ML evaluation |
| orders_dirty | Separate intentionally damaged subset | Five findings over four fault types; never used in training or clean reporting |

The procurement and cost modules share concepts, **not transaction amounts**. Do not join
their project populations or claim their totals reconcile. An integrated cost ledger, budget
history, contract documents, schedule activities and approved progress exports would be
required to make that stronger claim.

## Schema dictionary

The `columns` array in `data/manifest.json` lists every exported field. Key definitions:

- Dates: ISO calendar dates; no time-of-day ordering or timezone semantics.
- Monetary fields: fictional INR; no multi-currency aggregation. Amount = quantity × unit rate.
- `received_qty`: delivered quantity including later rejected quantities; a completed delivery
  means gross receipt quantity reaches order quantity, not acceptance of the entire order.
- `rejected_quantity`: subset of received quantity; does not trigger replacement orders here.
- `progress_assumption`: .25/.5/.75 synthetic physical progress, **not inferred from certificates**.
- `actual_cost`: simulated cumulative incurred cost; only in cash simulation is it assumed paid.
- `committed_remaining`: unspent committed amount, not cumulative commitment inclusive of actuals.
- `original_budget`: initial synthetic approved amount; no forecast or final-cost information.
- `approved_variations`, `pending_variations`: values known by snapshot under the simplified
  assumption that recorded status is established on variation date and does not change.
- `final_cost`, `completion_date`: outcome labels; excluded from predictors.
- `supplier_prior_late_rate`: smoothed rate `(late_completed + 1)/(completed + 2)`;
  only receipts completed before each order date enter history.

## Complexity, censoring and deliberate simplifications

Orders span 2022–2025; export cutoff is 2025-12-31. Later receipts, invoices and payments
are omitted. Incomplete orders are not labelled on-time. Packages start in 2019–2024;
only outcomes known at the cutoff are included. Partial delivery and instalment payments
produce one-to-many joins and realistic double-counting hazards.

Supplier effects, seasonality, material category and a 2025 delay shift generate delivery risk.
Unobserved complexity, pressure and random shocks generate package cost. Latent generating
factors are not exported as model features. Nevertheless synthetic signals are designed,
not discovered facts about construction. Simulated parameter uncertainty is not market evidence.

There are no people/HR records. No bank accounts, contact details or personal identifiers exist.
No claim is made that missing data, rare failures, supplier behaviour or extreme tails match reality.

## Before production use

Confirm permission, extract layouts, identifiers, record grains, cancellation/reversal rules,
currency/tax/retention treatment, event-time availability and label quality. Reconcile real exports,
capture repeated as-of snapshots and independently evaluate out-of-time and out-of-project.
Do not upload a client export to this public repository or a third-party model.

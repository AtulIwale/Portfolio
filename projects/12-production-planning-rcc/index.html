Rules, agreement & source integrity

Shortfall = (planned_qty − actual_qty) / planned_qty. Rework = rework_qty / actual_qty. Use unrounded ratios for decisions and show percentages to two decimals. The global classifier requires both shortfall ≥0.15 and rework ≥0.10, regardless of item ID. Labels are joined after classification, never used as classifier inputs.

Every threshold combination within 10–15% shortfall and 8–10% rework reproduces the same 70 IDs because of the numeric gaps above. Thresholds were selected after inspecting this dataset and its labels. This is deterministic, not a trained model; no independent discrimination, forecast or generalization is established.

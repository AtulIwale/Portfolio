Method, scope & dataset agreement

Confirmed category: exact join of fact_invoice.invoice_id to answer_key.invoice_id, selecting is_settlement_risk = Y. All 22 labeled invoices are unpaid and recorded as Overdue or Disputed. These are source labels, not independently detected or externally verified risks.

Repeat-pattern category: unpaid (blank payment_date) and recorded Overdue/Disputed, with at least two such invoices for the same account type and party in the full snapshot, excluding confirmed-category IDs. These 88 rows carry N in the settlement answer key. That N is not a manual-review ground-truth label; it does not validate or invalidate repeat-pattern review.

Agreement only: confirmed worklist compared with the same supplied Y labels: 22 matched positives, 0 extra positives, 0 missed positives; 978 remaining records. Precision = 22/22; recall = 22/22. Agreement is 100% by construction because category membership uses those labels. No repeat-pattern metric or combined metric is calculated.

Snapshot interpretation: synthetic ERP-style data; amounts in INR. Status is shown as recorded. No current-date aging, forecast, probability, weighted risk score or netting of AR and AP is performed. Repetition uses the complete snapshot, including the invoice being reviewed; it is not a historical prediction. Full invoice amounts are shown because partial-payment balances are not provided.

Integrity: Duplicate IDs, missing joins, invalid labels and inconsistent settlement status block rendering rather than being treated as clean. Opening a source ID shows the original invoice, label, party dimension and all related invoice records.

Refresh: the workbook is preserved unchanged in data/. This app embeds that snapshot for offline use; editing the workbook does not automatically update the app.

Inventory & warehouse management

Adjustment concentration with source evidence, plus a separate assumption-based stock review. Snapshot: 22 September 2026.

Confirmed adjustment-concentration risk

Answer-key agreement, not independent discrimination or model validation. Thresholds were selected after inspecting this dataset and its labels. Quantity ≤ −50 alone also reproduces all 120 Y labels; the concentration conditions add no discrimination in this workbook.

Stock-level review

assumption-based, not covered by answer_key

Unscored, proactive manual review.

Below-reorder comparisons require user-entered stock quantities. Staleness uses 90+ days without movement. Duplicate and reversal candidates require investigation. No negative-balance calculation or blended score.

Headline agreement uses all 1,200 transactions. Filters affect worklists only; concentration is always calculated across the full snapshot and all warehouses.

Confirmed adjustment worklist

Adjustment ≤ −50; at least two negative adjustments for the item/recorder pair; pair share ≥40% of that item’s total absolute negative-adjustment quantity. No hard-coded item or employee IDs.

Below-reorder review

assumption-based, not covered by answer_key · Unscored

The workbook has no opening balances, on-hand quantities or reorder points. Enter an as-of-2026-09-22 on-hand quantity below. The starting reorder threshold is an assumption of 10 units in that item’s unit of measure; replace it with your supplied threshold. Transactions are never converted into an invented balance.

Strictly below threshold triggers review; equality does not. Each entry applies only to the selected item × warehouse. Entries stay in this session and clear on reload. Negative quantities are not accepted; no negative-balance check is performed.

Stale item × warehouse review

assumption-based, not covered by answer_key · Unscored

At least 90 calendar days since the latest nonzero transaction, as of 2026-09-22. Any nonzero transaction, including an adjustment, counts as movement. Only item/warehouse combinations present in the workbook are evaluated; staleness does not establish stock remaining on hand.

Possible duplicate transactions

assumption-based, not covered by answer_key · Unscored

Different transaction IDs with identical item, warehouse, type, signed source quantity, date and recorder. Legitimate repeated movements can match this pattern. Every member of a matching group is shown.

Possible reversed transactions

assumption-based, not covered by answer_key · Unscored

Same item × warehouse, equal-and-opposite movement effects within an assumed seven calendar days (inclusive). Inward = +|quantity|, Outward = −|quantity|, Adjustment = signed quantity. Recorder may differ; all matching pairs are shown, without claiming one-to-one reversal allocation. No reversal-reference field exists.

Method, validation & source integrity

Deterministic, not a trained model. The confirmed classifier reads transaction fields only. The answer key is joined afterward to calculate agreement; it is not an input to the rule. Same-dataset threshold selection means this is not independent validation.

There is no validated target for below-reorder, stale, duplicate or reversed-transaction reviews. No precision or recall is assigned to any of them. Review units differ (stock positions, transaction rows, transaction pairs), and worklists may overlap with each other and confirmed flags. Counts are not added into a total risk score.

Raw Outward quantities are positive even though the dictionary says negative quantities reduce stock. Only reversal matching uses the explicit type-based movement convention above. No stock balance or net quantity across different items/units is calculated.

Dates and full-snapshot recorder shares describe this dataset, not a point-in-time forecast. Editing the workbook does not refresh embedded JSON. Source workbook is unchanged.

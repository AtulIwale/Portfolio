Deterministic, not a trained model. Each category is evaluated separately. Answer-key agreement describes this labeled dataset, not independent validation or performance on unseen data.

Rules, validation and source integrity

Rules use attendance fields only. The answer key is joined afterward by row_id; each category is compared with Y rows bearing its exact anomaly_type. The other two categories count as negatives for that category.

Elapsed duration = clock_out − clock_in, with fractional seconds preserved, no break deduction and no overnight rollover. The source has zero clock-out-earlier-than-clock-in rows. All 67 labeled excessive-duration rows are exactly 14.25 hours. The maximum N-labeled duration is 10.6790883333 hours, displayed as 10.68 hours. The inclusive threshold is 12 hours, applied before rounding.

“Excessive Overtime” is the dataset's duration-based category, not a finding about payable overtime or legal limits. Pending leave is an administrative review flag, not employee misconduct. There is no Rejected status in this workbook.

Missing or duplicate IDs, incomplete labels, invalid dates/times, unexpected statuses, overnight rows or disagreement with the audited snapshot block display. No unsupported proactive checks or thresholds are added. No automated payroll changes or live integrations.

All three source sheets are embedded. Editing the workbook does not update this snapshot automatically; data must be re-extracted and revalidated.

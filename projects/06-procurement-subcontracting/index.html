Reading the evidence

Days late is actual delivery date minus promised date, calculated in whole calendar days. Vendor history counts any positive delay. Ratings and categories provide context; they do not affect the score. The score is a ranking measure, not a probability, forecast or financial exposure estimate.

All 800 POs have delivery dates. Supplier history spans the complete snapshot and can include deliveries later than an individual PO. It is not a point-in-time forecasting feature. The workbook contains 80 severe delays (18–55 days), 476 minor delays (1–9 days) and 244 on-time or early deliveries.

The unchanged synthetic workbook is embedded in this file. Editing the Excel source requires refreshing the embedded snapshot. No orders, supplier ratings or payments are changed by this app.
